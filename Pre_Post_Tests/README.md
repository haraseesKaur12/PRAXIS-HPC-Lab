# PRAXIS – Pre-Test and Post-Test Assessments

This folder contains the pre-test and post-test question papers used in the CS565 High Performance Computing Laboratory course at IIT Ropar.

The assessments were designed using Bloom's Taxonomy to evaluate students' conceptual understanding, practical HPC knowledge, and higher-order reasoning skills.

## Pre-Test

The pre-test assesses students' foundational understanding of high-performance computing, MPI communication, collective operations, communication performance, and scalability before laboratory instruction.

## Post-Test

The post-test assesses students' understanding after completing the laboratory modules, with emphasis on MPI programming, collective communication, performance modeling, synchronization, simulation, scalability, and optimization.

## Assessment Materials

- `Pre_Test.pdf` – Pre-test question paper
- `Post_Test.pdf` – Post-test question paper

These materials support the educational methodology presented in the PRAXIS research paper.
